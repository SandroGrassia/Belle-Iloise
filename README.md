# Belle-Iloise
### Project for an midi Talking Drum

this midi controller, that I made in 2017, is designed to be used as a talking drum.
Sound is triggered by tapping on the round pad, the pitch is modulated by squeezing the clothespin.
On the top side, three knobs control the central note, the pitch span and the volume. On the bottom side there is the on/off switch and the MIDI-OUT socket.
The device is powered by two 2x1.5V AA batteries; a small voltage regulator steps the voltage up to 5V required by the circuit.
